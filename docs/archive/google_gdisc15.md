# Archived Google GDISC15 Diagnostics

GDISC13/GDISC15, `local_full`, `proxy_feature_ablation`, the old Google
DEM/static runner and summarizer, the X/Z scorecard, the benchmark suite, and
S3E are archived as a historical Google DEM-proxy diagnostic stack. They
reported predictive utility and proxy-structure diagnostics only; they were not
the Google real-data teacher-learner mainline and did not recover true physical
mechanisms from Google hardware data.

Archived code lives under `src/scope_static/archive/google_gdisc15/` and
`src/scope_static/archive/experiments/google_gdisc15/`. Archived configs live
under `configs/archive/google_gdisc15/`, archived tests under
`tests/archive/google_gdisc15/`, and archived outputs under
`outputs/archive/google_gdisc15/`.
