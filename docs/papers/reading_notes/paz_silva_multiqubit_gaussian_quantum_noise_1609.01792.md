## Executive summary [paper]
The paper introduces **multi-pulse quantum noise spectroscopy (QNS)** protocols that, using N
qubits as *actively-controlled probes*, reconstruct **all** auto- and cross-correlation spectra of
a Gaussian **dephasing** bath — including the **asymmetric "quantum" spectra** `S⁻(ω)` that a
single qubit cannot see. `===== PAGE 1 =====`

> "We introduce multi-pulse quantum noise spectroscopy protocols for spectral estimation of the
> noise affecting multiple qubits coupled to Gaussian dephasing environments including both
> classical and quantum sources. Our protocols are capable of reconstructing all the noise auto- and
> cross-correlation spectra entering the multiqubit dynamics." `===== PAGE 1 =====`

The mechanism: apply designed sequences of **π-pulses (and, for the general case, SWAP gates)**,
repeat a base sequence M≫1 times to engineer a **frequency comb** in the filter functions, prepare
the qubits in known states, **measure X/Y expectation values**, and **linearly invert** the
resulting deconvolution. Numerically demonstrated on two exciton qubits in a 1D phonon bath
(reconstructed `S⁺`, `Re/Im S±_{1,2}` at 33 harmonics; recovered TB = 5.02 K vs. 5 K actual and
J(ω)). `===== PAGE 19 =====`

## Method (deep) [paper]

### Open-system model — dephasing only `===== PAGE 3-4 =====`
Interaction-picture Hamiltonian (Eq. 1), purely dephasing `[H_S, H_SB] = 0`:

    H_I(t) = Σ_ℓ Z_ℓ ⊗ B_ℓ(t) + Σ_{ℓ≠ℓ'} Z_{ℓℓ'} ⊗ B_{ℓℓ'}(t)          (Eq. 1)

with `B_ℓ(t) = ζ_ℓ(t)·1 + B̃_ℓ(t)` (Eq. 2): `ζ` = classical stochastic process, `B̃` = quantum
bath operator. Two coupling classes: **M1** (full-rank Pauli coupling, `B_0=0`) and **M2**
(rank-1 projector coupling, `B_0 = Σ_ℓ B_ℓ ≠ 0`, e.g. exciton qubits). `===== PAGE 4 =====`

### Spectra — the classical (`+`) vs quantum (`-`) decomposition `===== PAGE 5-6 =====`
Power spectrum = Fourier transform of the 2nd cumulant over lag τ (Eq. 10):

    S_{a,b}(ω) = ∫ dτ e^{-iωτ} C^{(2)}(B_a(τ), B_b(0))                    (Eq. 10)

**Quantum spectrum (antisymmetric / commutator)** (Eqs. 11-12):

    S⁻_{a,b}(ω) ≡ ∫ dτ e^{-iωτ} ⟨[B_a(τ), B_b(0)]⟩_q = S_{a,b}(ω) − S_{b,a}(−ω)

**Classical spectrum (symmetric / anti-commutator)** (Eqs. 13-14):

    S⁺_{a,b}(ω) ≡ ∫ dτ e^{-iωτ} ⟨{B_a(τ), B_b(0)}⟩_{c,q} = S_{a,b}(ω) + S_{b,a}(−ω)

The self/cross split is a=b vs a≠b. Bosonic thermal case (Eqs. 18-19):
`S⁺ = 2πJ coth(β|ω|/2)`, `S⁻ = 2πJ sign(ω)` — so `S⁺/S⁻ = coth(βω/2)` gives temperature.
`===== PAGE 6 =====`

### Exact reduced dynamics — the Theorem `===== PAGE 6-7 =====`
For a factorizable initial state and a dephasing-preserving invertible observable O, the exact
controlled expectation value is (Eq. 22):

    E_ρ0(O(t)) = Tr[ e^{ -i C⁽¹⁾_O(t) − C⁽²⁾_O(t)/2! } ρ0 O ]              (Eq. 22)

with the 1st/2nd time-dependent cumulants (Eqs. 23-24). Zero-mean ⇒ `C⁽¹⁾=0`; the dynamics are
governed by the **2nd-order** cumulant `C⁽²⁾_O(t)` (Eq. 25 in time domain; Eq. 27 in frequency
domain), which is a **convolution of the bath spectra `S±` with control filter functions
`G±_{a,a';b,b'}(ω,t)`** built from 1st/2nd fundamental FFs (Eqs. 8-9).

> "Eq. (27) makes it explicit that the reduced qubit dynamics are completely ruled by convolutions
> between the power spectra of the bath and the FFs generated by external control." `===== PAGE 7 =====`

### The protocol — active control + repeated tomography + comb inversion `===== PAGE 9-13 =====`
Four steps (verbatim below in OBSERVABLE section): prepare known state → evolve under noise +
**external control** → **measure** observables → deconvolve. Deconvolution uses the Álvarez-Suter
frequency-comb trick: repeating a base control sequence M times sharpens `G⁺(ω,MT)` into a comb of
δ-teeth at harmonics `kω₀ = k·2π/T`, discretizing the convolution into a **linear system solved by
inversion** over `Nc ≥ |K|` distinct control sequences. `===== PAGE 10 =====` Two-qubit setting:
prepare the states / measure the observables in Table I (X₁,Y₁,X₂,Y₂,X₁X₂,Y₁Y₂,Y₁X₂,X₁Y₂); the
`A±`, `B±`, `D±` combinations (built from `E[X]`,`E[Y]`) yield all expansion coefficients
`C_{ℓ,a}` (Eqs. 51-56). `===== PAGE 13 =====`

## The MECHANISM (for implementation) [paper → ours]
- **Model** = Gaussian dephasing bath, `H_I = Σ Z_ℓ ⊗ B_ℓ + Σ Z_{ℓℓ'} ⊗ B_{ℓℓ'}` (Eq. 1),
  with a bosonic `B_ℓ(t) = Σ_k (e^{iΩ_k t} g_ℓk a†_k + h.c.)` (Eq. 3). Classical + quantum sources
  additively separate at the 2nd-cumulant level. [paper]
- **Grounded params** (numerical study): Ohmic 1D phonon bath `J(ω)=ξ|ω|e^{-ω²/ω_c²}`, ξ=0.001,
  ω_c=1.5 THz, qubit separation 10 nm, v_s=7 km/s, TB=5 K, max cycle T=60 ps, min switching
  τ₀=0.2 ps, 33 harmonics. `===== PAGE 19 =====` [paper]
- **This is an ACTIVE-CONTROL spectroscopy mechanism**, not a passive record-statistics model.
  [ours] It is not directly a QEC-teacher channel; it is the *prior-art owner* of the
  asymmetric-spectrum concept and of continuous-Gaussian-covariance identifiability *via control*.

## The OBSERVABLE / metric [paper]
The observable is **actively-controlled probe-qubit state tomography**. The four-step protocol,
verbatim:

> "(1) prepare the qubits in a known state; (2) let the qubits evolve under both bath-induced noise
> and external control; (3) measure a set of observables on the qubits that quantifies their
> response to the bath and control; (4) extract information about the bath spectra from the measured
> value of the observables." `===== PAGE 9 =====`

Concretely for one qubit: prepare `|+⟩`, evolve under control for time T, **repeatedly measure X₁
or Y₁**, and form `E[X₁(T)]² + E[Y₁(T)]² = e^{-2χ(T)}` (Eq. 39), where χ is the classical-spectrum
convolution (Eq. 40). `===== PAGE 9 =====` Two-qubit: state preparations + Pauli observables of
Table I. `===== PAGE 13 =====`

- **INSUFFICIENT statistic flagged:** a single qubit in a generic dephasing setting sees only the
  **even** part `S⁺_{1,1}(ω) = S(ω)+S(−ω)`; the quantum self-spectrum `S⁻_{ℓ,ℓ}` is undetected by
  single-qubit QNS. Multiqubit control is what unlocks the asymmetric part. `===== PAGE 2 =====`
- The lever is **control-timing symmetry** (displacement/mirror (anti)symmetry) engineering the FF
  combs — an *active* resource, not a measurement-order effect. `===== PAGE 10-11 =====`

## Findings + numbers [paper]
- Complete two-qubit reconstruction: `S⁺_{1,1}`, `S⁺_{2,2}`, `Re[S±_{1,2}]`, `Im[S±_{1,2}]` at
  |K|=33 harmonics, "excellent agreement with the actual spectra." Quantum self-spectra `S⁻_{ℓ,ℓ}`
  recovered *indirectly* under bosonic prior via Eq. 73. `===== PAGE 19 =====`
- Thermometry: `S⁺/S⁻ → coth(βω/2)` → estimated **TB ≈ 5.02 K vs 5 K actual**; J(ω) recovered.
  `===== PAGE 19 =====`
- Ignoring the quantum spectra mis-predicts dynamics: worst-case fidelity error up to **11%** (free
  evolution), ~2% under DD. `===== PAGE 20-21 =====`
- **Order structure:** the reconstructed object is the **2nd-order cumulant** `C⁽²⁾_O` (Eqs. 22-27) —
  i.e. **second order in the system-bath COUPLING B (the filter-function/Dyson-Magnus order)**.
  There is **no** g⁴ / order-in-MEASUREMENT structure; measurement is repeated projective tomography
  that estimates expectation values, and the expansion order is in the control-coupling convolution.
  [paper, Eqs. 22-27 + PAGE 12 expansion Eq. 49] [ours: the "2nd order" is order-in-coupling, NOT
  order-in-measurement-record]

## Limitations [paper]
- **Dephasing only** (`[H_S,H_SB]=0`); relaxation/non-commuting noise excluded (`===== PAGE 3 =====`).
  Steady-state relaxation discussed only heuristically via the interpolating model.
- **Gaussian statistics** assumed (only 2nd cumulant nonzero); non-Gaussian noise out of scope
  (deferred to Ref. [14]). `===== PAGE 5 =====`
- **Stationary** bath (time-translation invariant correlations). `===== PAGE 6 =====`
- **Perfect, instantaneous control** (idealized π-pulses / SWAP gates). `===== PAGE 4 =====`
- Quantum **self**-spectra `S⁻_{ℓ,ℓ}` need SWAP (non-local) control *or* a bosonic-bath prior; local
  π-pulses alone reconstruct all others. `===== PAGE 3, 6 =====`
- Numerical demonstration only (two exciton qubits); no hardware data.

## Relevance to qec_twin [ours]

### BONE A — "asymmetry = quantum part of noise"
**Owned, at the level of the DEFINITION and of ACTIVE-CONTROL spectroscopy.** The paper names
`S⁻_{a,b}(ω) = S_{a,b}(ω) − S_{b,a}(−ω)` the "quantum spectrum" and proves it "is non-zero only when
the bath is quantum" (commutator vanishes classically). `===== PAGE 6 =====` So the *concept* "the
antisymmetric/asymmetric spectrum is the non-classical part of noise" is prior art here. **BUT** the
access channel is **active pulse-sequence control + probe-qubit tomography + frequency-comb linear
inversion** — NOT projective/stabilizer **measurement records**, and NOT any g⁴ /
order-in-measurement structure. The expansion is 2nd-order-in-COUPLING (Eqs. 22-27). So it does
**not** pre-empt a claim about accessing S⁻ (or its analogue) from a **passive
QEC-detector/measurement-record** channel, nor any measurement-order (g⁴) structure. Our
CGF-probe branch-B "asymmetry KILLED under passive record law" finding is in a genuinely different
observable class from this paper's control-based access.

### BONE B — continuous Gaussian multiqubit covariance identifiability
**Owned for the ACTIVE-CONTROL observable, and only there.** The paper reconstructs the *entire*
set of continuous auto- + cross-spectra (`S⁺`, `S⁻`, real+imag) of an N-qubit Gaussian dephasing
bath — a full continuous cross-spectrum/covariance identifiability result. `===== PAGE 1, 19 =====`
The identifying observable is **prepared-state qubit tomography under engineered control sequences**
(Table I, Eq. 39, four-step protocol) — categorically **active control**, not passive
stabilizer/detector moments. Therefore it does **NOT** own a **passive-detector-moment
identifiability map**: nothing in the paper reconstructs spectra from *un-controlled repeated
syndrome/detector records*. That passive-record identifiability question is left open by this paper.

### What to REUSE
- The `S⁺`/`S⁻` (anti-commutator/commutator) spectral vocabulary and the `S⁺/S⁻ = coth(βω/2)`
  temperature identity — a clean, field-standard framing of "quantum part of noise" to cite when we
  need to say what our passive-record probe can/can't see, and to sharpen the control-vs-record
  distinction.
- The exact Gaussian-dephasing cumulant theorem (Eqs. 22-24) as an independent closed-form check for
  any Gaussian-dephasing teacher slice we build.

### CORRECTION it forces
Reinforces the memory-note discipline that **the observable must be pinned**: "asymmetric spectrum =
quantum noise" is a *definition* that is prior art, but "we can read it from **passive records**" is
NOT owned by this paper (they use active control). Do not let a reviewer collapse the two — cite the
control-vs-record distinction explicitly.
