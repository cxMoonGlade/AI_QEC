## Executive summary [paper]
Provides an **explicit constructive parameterization** of an arbitrary n-qubit unitary from single-qubit gates
+ one fixed two-qubit (coupling) gate, via **successive Cartan decompositions** of SU(2ⁿ) using the Riemannian
symmetric space SU(2ⁿ)/(SU(2ⁿ⁻¹)⊗SU(2ⁿ⁻¹)⊗U(1)). The n=2 base case is the two-qubit KAK decomposition; the
recursion extends it to spin networks (worked for n=2,3), with the coupling I_zI_z driving all required Cartan
generators via Weyl orbits.

## Method (deep) [paper]
**Product-operator basis (Sec. 3, Notation 2 / Example 1).** Iα = ½σα (Pauli/2). For n=2, su(4) basis:
- q=1 (local): i{I₁ₓ, I₁ᵧ, I₁_z, I₂ₓ, I₂ᵧ, I₂_z}.
- **q=2 (two-body / non-local): i{I₁ₓI₂ₓ, I₁ₓI₂ᵧ, I₁ₓI₂_z, I₁ᵧI₂ₓ, I₁ᵧI₂ᵧ, I₁ᵧI₂_z, I₁_zI₂ₓ, I₁_zI₂ᵧ,
  I₁_zI₂_z}** — **the 9 Pauli⊗Pauli operators**, the non-local part m of su(4).

**Cartan decomposition of su(4) (Sec. 4, Example 2).** g = su(4) = m ⊕ k with
- k = span i{I₁ₓ, I₁ᵧ, I₁_z, I₂ₓ, I₂ᵧ, I₂_z}  (local, generates K = SU(2)⊗SU(2)),
- **m = span i{I₁ₓI₂ₓ, I₁ₓI₂ᵧ, I₁ₓI₂_z, I₁ᵧI₂ₓ, I₁ᵧI₂ᵧ, I₁ᵧI₂_z, I₁_zI₂ₓ, I₁_zI₂ᵧ, I₁_zI₂_z}** (non-local),

satisfying [k,k]⊂k, [m,k]=m, [m,m]⊂k. The **maximal Abelian (Cartan) subalgebra**:
> **h = span i{I₁ₓI₂ₓ, I₁ᵧI₂ᵧ, I₁_zI₂_z}**   (Sec. 4, "the Abelian subalgebra h generated by …")

= **{XX, YY, ZZ}**. Hence (Corollary 1) any U_F ∈ SU(4) decomposes:
> **U_F = K₁ exp(-i(α₁ I₁ₓI₂ₓ + α₂ I₁ᵧI₂ᵧ + α₃ I₁_zI₂_z)) K₂**,  K₁,K₂ ∈ SU(2)⊗SU(2).   (Sec. 4)

**Weyl-orbit construction (Remark 3, Eq. of Sec. 4).** Starting from a single coupling I₁_zI₂_z, the other two
Cartan generators are produced by local conjugation:
> K_y exp(-iα₁ I₁_zI₂_z) K_y⁻¹ = exp(-iα₁ **I₁ₓI₂ₓ**),  with K_y = exp(-i(π/2)I₁ᵧ)exp(-i(π/2)I₂ᵧ);
> K_x⁻¹ exp(-iα₂ I₁_zI₂_z) K_x = exp(-iα₂ **I₁ᵧI₂ᵧ**),  with K_x = exp(-i(π/2)I₁ₓ)exp(-i(π/2)I₂ₓ).

> **Remark 3:** "the Hamiltonians I₁ₓI₂ₓ and I₁ᵧI₂ᵧ are the elements of Weyl orbit of I₁_zI₂_z under the
> adjoint action of SU(2)⊗SU(2)." — XX, YY, ZZ are one Weyl orbit ⇒ canonical equivalent generators.

## Findings + numbers [paper]
- su(4) = m ⊕ k Cartan decomposition; Cartan subalgebra h = {XX,YY,ZZ}; U_F = K₁ exp(-i Σ α_β β⊗β) K₂.
- Recursion to n qubits: U ∈ SU(2ⁿ) factors into ≤ a fixed number of two-qubit coupling evolutions + local
  gates (Corollary 3; worked for n=3, Example 4). The two-qubit case SU(4)/SU(2)⊗SU(2) is a Riemannian
  symmetric space ⇒ the n=2 KAK is time-optimal (Remark 3, citing Khaneja–Brockett–Glaser).

## Limitations [paper]
- Closed unitary synthesis only (NMR coherent control); no dissipation/leakage/CPTP. The "two-body
  interaction" is the *coupling Hamiltonian* algebra — exactly the coherent content we ground; noise is a
  separate carrier arm.
- n>2 is not time-optimal (the coset space loses the symmetric-space structure). Irrelevant to our 2-body
  M22 grounding.
