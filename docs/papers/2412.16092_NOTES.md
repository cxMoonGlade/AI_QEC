## What they do

Hybrid gate-level noise model for IBM transmons (39 qubits, 7 IBMQP devices):

- **Model**: LME on H_D ⊗ H_Sp ⊗ H_TLS. Three noise tiers:
  1. *Locally Markovian*: GAD (T1, thermal q), phase damping (T_φ), bit-flip control
     dissipation during x-rotations; SPAM as pre-measurement bit-flip channels.
  2. *Extended Markovian*: spatial correlations by ENLARGING the quantum system —
     always-on ZZ crosstalk (qubit–qubit J_ij) + qubit–TLS ZZ couplings (ξ_jk).
  3. *Stochastic* (the non-Markovian part): classical Gaussian wide-sense-stationary
     processes ε_j(t) (control) and β_j(t) (dephasing) in a stochastic Hamiltonian;
     characterized by PSDs via QNS/FTTPS (filter-function formalism); simulated by
     trajectory-averaging the LME.
- **Sparsity**: ~10 params/qubit + 3/pair. ECR two-qubit gate: over-rotation ε_zx,
  target detuning β, residual x ζ, ZZ J — and NO two-qubit dissipative terms needed
  (single-qubit incoherent suffices to match experiment — notable sparsity finding).
- **Protocol**: dedicated noise-amplification circuits C = {M, T1, T2, P, Q(FTTPS),
  XT, CR}; MSE fit on survival probabilities; model selection = extended-Markovian
  first, PROMOTE dephasing/control static→stochastic when RB prediction disagrees,
  then QNS extracts PSDs.
- **Validation**: RB rates, multi-qubit DD, VQE (H2): expectation values within 0.5%
  relative error — 7× better than IBM default noise models. "Training proxy for
  hardware" use-case.

## Relation to qec_twin (the wedge)

| Axis | Oda et al. | qec_twin |
|---|---|---|
| Object | gate-level ops + small algorithm circuits | QEC-scale error-mechanism field (d=29 rep-code, 1002 layers), decoder-facing DEM/CPTP twin |
| Access | INTERVENTIONAL: dedicated amplification + QNS probe circuits | OBSERVATIONAL, label-free, in-situ syndrome stream only (probe-richness C_cal(r) is the identifiability lever, not device access) |
| Non-Markovianity | classical colored noise (PSDs) + TLS quantum DOF | bunching spectrum on mechanisms (f̂ ≡ r̂·R̂, located per-window R̂), multi-lag R_k = paper-2/K2 axis |
| Validation | expectation values (0.5% rel.) | held-out syndrome NLL (+56/+44 nats vs SI1000), ΔLER under frozen decoder, identity-exact located findings |
| Platform | IBM (fixed-frequency, ECR) | Google Willow release (tunable, rep-code memory) |

- **Not a scooping threat** to paper-1's bunching chain: no QEC records, no DEMs, no
  decoders, no held-out likelihood on syndrome data. The clock-setter remains
  arXiv:2512.10814.
- **Useful citation** (related-work, characterization side): independent IBM-platform
  evidence that spatio-temporally correlated + TLS noise is real and dominant —
  corroborates the physical plausibility of our located bunching heterogeneity.
  Slots next to Chen et al. 2206.06362 (Pauli learnability) as the
  interventional/probe-rich end of the spectrum vs our observational/label-free end.
- **Methodological echoes**: (i) their extended-Markovian trick (add quantum DOF for
  spatial correlation) vs our ADR-0006 verdict (one declared edge DOF closes the
  φ-sensitive band) — same lesson, different formalism; (ii) their static→stochastic
  PROMOTION rule (promote only when RB prediction fails) is a clean protocol pattern
  for our M5 drift-axis pre-registrations; (iii) "no 2-qubit dissipative terms
  needed" = sparsity precedent for carrier design.
