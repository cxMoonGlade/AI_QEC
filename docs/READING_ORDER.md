## Current stage

The product boundary has moved from a route-centric “GPU-first full-Record carrier” contract to a
backend-neutral error-to-Record contract with separate capability and assurance claims. The
mathematical target remains the joint detector/observable Record law; a backend may expose it through
sampling, scoring, a named functional, a structured law, or complete enumeration.

No target solver has been selected or implemented by this boundary update. The current no-cutoff
target-lowering stage validated static target definitions only; separate exact micro-owners qualify
their frozen pair, ADD, and retained-boundary metric semantics. Complete target Record-law
certification remains unanchored, but that absence is no longer a product-wide prohibition on
building an experimental owner.

## Current facts

- The declared-error/Record boundary is retained. Device inference, bath reconstruction, decoder
  selection, and DEM reduction remain outside or downstream.
- Detector semantics are explicit parity incidence. The adjacent-round XOR helper is one supported
  layout profile.
- `SAMPLE`, `SCORE_RECORD`/`SCORE_PREFIX`, `STRUCTURED_LAW`, and `ENUMERATE_LAW` are different
  capabilities.
- `EXECUTION_ATTESTED`, `FAMILY_QUALIFIED`, `METRIC_CERTIFIED`, and
  `FULL_RECORD_LAW_CERTIFIED` are different assurance claims.
- Full joint-Record TV is the registered complete-law metric whose bound controls every bounded
  Record functional. It is not required to begin every experimental solver.
- Unbounded truncation cannot support faithful sampling of the original declared process. A local
  discarded weight still does not become a Record bound.
- `CORE/RESEARCH`, certification verdicts, metric epistemic classes, provenance kinds, and workflow
  permissions remain distinct vocabularies.
- Historical preregistrations and result artifacts are not rewritten. Their hashes and
  `solver_permission=CODE_BLOCKED` fields continue to describe their frozen plans.

## Immediate handoff

The next solver decision, if authorized, must begin from the validated static target programs and
produce one experimental owner with:

1. a capability manifest and exact support predicate;
2. a pre-execution support decision;
3. an execution manifest and complete numerical-error ledger;
4. independent primitive/lowering checks, small complete Record fixtures, and persistent-versus-IID
   corruption controls;
5. explicit separation of family qualification from target-instance metric or full-law claims.

This does not predetermine pair recurrence, dynamic ADD, retained-boundary TN, FLO, stabilizer-rank,
or another architecture. Static target objects and discriminating measurements should decide among
them. Any `src/**` phase still requires explicit user confirmation and a reviewed phase diff.

## Do not inherit as design premises

- “GPU-first” as a product requirement;
- PEPS, PEPO, MPS, GCAMPS, ADD, pair recurrence, or any other carrier as the product definition;
- adjacent-round XOR as the only detector layout;
- a target-size dense probability mass function as the only valid Record interface;
- absence of a target-size full-law oracle as a permanent ban on experimental implementation;
- `CORE/RESEARCH` or `PASS/UNANCHORED` as a substitute for capability or assurance scope;
- old route histories, benchmark values, or literature caches as proof of a field-wide gap.
