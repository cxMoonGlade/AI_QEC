# No-cutoff structure census — literature closure packet

## Frozen claim

`Decision/consequence:` before any new exact simulator route is written, run a
route-qualified structure census on one neutral `d in {3,5}` × increasing-round
fixture and keep new solver work `CODE_BLOCKED` until the falsifier is frozen.
`Mechanism:` a caller-declared binary coherent sign is sampled once per shot
and persists across every declared non-Clifford error location; this is an error
process, not a microscopic bath or open-system model.  `Record object:` the
complete chronological detector bits plus the logical-observable bit.
`Bridge under test:` the exact representation burden of four architecture
hypotheses is described respectively by headline
`2^k_max_clifft_squeeze_no_peephole`, the peak exact Pauli-pair reachable-state
count, the peak live-node count of a dynamic exact
ADD over the same pair-state recurrence, and the mixed-domain peak dense-bucket
capacity of a frozen factorized retained-boundary tensor route.  The raw
project boundary width is reported separately and is not substituted into
`2^tw`. SymFT's separate width, monolithic burden, and current exact
product-component capacities are supplemental implementation-qualified
diagnostics. `Correctness condition:` the complete Record-law TV distance to
an independent oracle must be reported, and unavailable certification remains
unavailable rather than becoming zero.  `Prediction:` none of the four routes
has a literature-grounded scaling winner; finite-grid growth is a prospective
architecture falsifier, not an asymptotic theorem.  `No-go:` structural
disposition and Record-law faithfulness are independent: the registered burden
rule may reject a pinned representation, but absent target TV keeps every new
solver `CODE_BLOCKED`.  `Implementation target:` documentation,
fixture/schema instrumentation, external compile-only adapters, and tests;
not a new solver and no `src/**` change.
